# Thumbnail image credits

The thumbnail is a composite of real astronomical observation images with the existing Razi More Asteroids dragon graphic. No generative AI was used.

**Nebula background:** *Webb Takes a Stunning, Star-Filled Portrait of the Pillars of Creation (Cropped)*, https://esawebb.org/images/weic2216a/

**Required Webb image credit:** NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI; J. DePasquale, A. Koekemoer, A. Pagan (STScI).

**Webb image license:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

**Asteroid:** NASA's Bennu/OSIRIS-REx observational imagery, NASA/Goddard/University of Arizona, https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/10-things-whats-that-space-rock/

**Changes:** square background crop and subdued brightness, black photographic backdrop removed from the asteroid image, composited and resized, with the unchanged Razi More Asteroids dragon logo in a lower-left panel.
