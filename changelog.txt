Date: 22. 10. 2026

    - Refine all 56 icons and 40 asteroid textures for cleaner facets and readable mineral details.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 21. 10. 2026
  Major Features:
    - First public-ready standalone fork of More Asteroids for Space Age 2.1.
    - Requires RAZI Library 1.3.1 for shared recipe, technology and settings helpers.
    - Adds six themed asteroid families, including quartz-rich crystalline asteroids.
  Compatibility:
    - Space Age works without Krastorio 2: Spaced Out.
    - Optional K2SO processing adds eight advanced orbital resource recipes.
    - Dry asteroid recipes work in compatible K2SO crushers.
    - Other mods retain ownership of their planets and space connections.
  Graphics:
    - Retouched all original asteroid graphics to match their resources.
    - Standardized in-game icons to 64 pixels and thumbnail to 144 pixels.
  Bugfixes:
    - Updated deprecated recipe fields for Factorio 2.1.
    - Removed obsolete parameter prototypes and vanilla asteroid-light mutations.
    - Corrected source graphics and removed unused copies.
  Info:
    - Credits and MIT license retained from the original More Asteroids mod.
